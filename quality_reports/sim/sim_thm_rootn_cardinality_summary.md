# thm:rootn at $\mathsf L = 4$: the limit law on the paper's own design

Scope S1. Produced by `scripts/simulations/sim_thm_rootn_cardinality.R`, seed 20260814, on 8 cores.
Certificate: `scripts/simulations/sim_thm_rootn_l2identity.R`. Raw cells: `sim_thm_rootn_cardinality.rds`.

## What was tested

`thm:rootn`'s limit law, $\sqrt n(\hat\theta-\theta_\dagger)=J^{-1}n^{-1/2}\sum_i\psi_\star(O_i)+o_P(1)\to N(0,J^{-1}\Sigma J^{-1\prime})$,
at instrument cardinality $\mathsf L\in\{2,4\}$. The theorem is stated at general finite $\mathsf L$; §5's
evidence was binary on both designs. Four statistics per cell: coverage of Wald intervals, quantiles of the
studentised statistic against $N(0,1)$, $\hat V\to V$, and the **linearisation residual**
$\sqrt n(\hat\theta-\theta_\dagger)-J^{-1}\sqrt n\,\mathbb E_n\psi_\star$, which `eq:rootn` asserts is $o_P(1)$
and which is the sharpest of the four.

## DGPs, grid, replications

DGP-A (favourable), frontier-clearing configuration $(\mu,s,a,\alpha)=(2,3,\tfrac34,0.32)$, $p=4$, $d_Z=3$.
$n\in\{1000,8000,64000\}$, $B=5000$ at **every** cell. Nuisances **injected** at the theorem's own rates at
both cardinalities, $c_\delta=0.25$, $c_\epsilon=c_r=1$, smooth $h$ geometry, $h_{\text{bias}}=0$.

**One factor varies.** $c_\delta$, $c_\epsilon$, $c_r$, the $n$ grid, $B$, the overlap floor
$\varepsilon_\varrho=0.10$, the ridge $10^{-10}$ in the population balancing solve, and the nuisance regime are
identical across the two panels. Realised injected sizes agree across $\mathsf L$ to within 1% ($\|h\|$
identical by construction, $\|\hat\ell-\ell_\star\|$ within 0.6%, $\|\hat\varrho-\varrho\|$ within 11% and
*smaller* at $\mathsf L=4$). What $\mathsf L$ forces — three propensity indices instead of one, four
first-stage shifts instead of two — is generated by a rule that returns the shipped binary design at
$\mathsf L=2$ (design decision D8).

**Binary identity.** 36/36 objects `identical()` against the vendored pre-cardinality sources, including
`one_rep_full` and the $d_Z=1$ headroom path; the budget and Jacobian arms reproduce their `.tex` byte for
byte; the $\mathsf L=2$ design-validity numbers reproduce `sim_thm_rootn_dgpvalid.tex` exactly.

## Where the failure is, and where it is not

Ablation at $B=1000$, independent streams (gaps are 20–100 MC SE, so the cross-stream comparison is safe):

| arm | $\mathsf L=2$ residual | $\mathsf L=4$ residual |
|---|---|---|
| all exact ($c_\delta=c_\epsilon=c_r=0$) | 0.149 → 0.053 → 0.019 | 0.178 → 0.062 → 0.022 |
| $h$ only ($\hat\ell=\ell_\star$, $\hat\varrho=\varrho$) | 0.597 → 0.412 → 0.287 | 0.657 → 0.455 → 0.328 |
| full (shipped arm, $B=5000$) | 0.657 → 0.474 → 0.363 | 0.856 → 0.909 → 1.230 |

With perfect nuisances the $\mathsf L=4$ estimator linearises at the **same rate** as the binary one, and
95% coverage is 0.940. With only the structural error injected it again falls at the same rate, coverage
0.956. The divergence appears **only** when $\hat\ell\neq\ell_\star$ and $\hat\varrho\neq\varrho$ are
switched on. So it is not the $\mathsf L\ge3$ machinery and not the $\varphi$-error term.

**The four remainder products the theorem prices are matched across cardinalities** —
$\sqrt n\delta_n^2$ identical, $\sqrt n\|h\|\|\hat\ell-\ell_\star\|$ within 0.8%,
$\sqrt n r_n\|h\|$ and $\sqrt n r_n\|\hat L-L_\star\|$ within 11% and *smaller* at $\mathsf L=4$ — while
the residual ratio $\mathsf L{=}4/\mathsf L{=}2$ grows $1.30\to1.92\to3.39$. The failure is therefore in the
**constant multiplying** those products, not in the products. $\sup|\ell_\star|$ rises 3.04 → 7.56 and
$\lambda_{\min}(\Omega)$ falls 0.0933 → 0.0273 (against its own floor $\varepsilon_\varrho/\mathsf L$, which it
clears).

Hypothesis for the theorist, offered as a hypothesis and not a result: A5(iv) prices
$\|\hat\ell-\ell_\star\|_{L^2(P)}$ in the **Frobenius** norm, but $\psi$ and $\hat J$ consume the
*contraction* $\hat\ell\widetilde W=\sum_l\hat\ell_{\cdot l}\widetilde W_l$, and at $\mathsf L\ge3$
$\Omega=\operatorname{diag}(\varrho)-\varrho\varrho'$ is not diagonal. A Frobenius bound does not control a
contraction against correlated contrasts uniformly in $\mathsf L$. `assumptions_main.tex` already records
that $\Omega$ "enters as the conditional covariance and never as a weight" and that
$\widetilde W^\Omega:=\Omega^{-1}\widetilde W$ "exists and is worth knowing"; this is that seam.

> **REFUTED 2026-08-15 by S2, which measured both norms.** The ratio consumed/priced —
> $\|\sum_l(\hat\ell-\ell_\star)_{\cdot l}\widetilde W_l\|_{L^2(P)}$ over $\|\hat\ell-\ell_\star\|_F$ — is
> 0.463 at $\mathsf L=2$ and 0.420 at $\mathsf L=4$, flat in $n$ and **smaller** at the higher cardinality.
> A5(iv)'s Frobenius norm is conservative there, not slack, so the proposed seam is not in this design and
> the measurement moves the wrong way for the hypothesis. S2 locates the failure in the **first-order
> propensity term** $\sqrt n\,\mathbb E_n[L_\star(\hat\varrho-\varrho)]$, which grows at $n^{+0.189}$ at
> $\mathsf L=4$ against the $n^{1/2-\alpha}=n^{+0.180}$ a non-orthogonal first-order term would show, and
> decays at $\mathsf L=2$. The seam is in the moment's $\varrho$-derivative, not in the norm A5(iv) uses.
