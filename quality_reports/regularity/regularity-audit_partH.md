## C1 [CRITICAL] — the source-condition conversion is unlicensed at $2\mu>1$, and Part H lives there by construction
`proofs.tex:4061–4070`.

(L5) claims (P-a) puts $\bar v_\dagger$ in $\mathcal R\big((\hat{\mathcal A}^\ast\hat{\mathcal A})^\mu\big)$ with pre-image norm $O_P(\mathsf R)$. **(P-a) supplies two ingredients** — (a) $\|\bar v_\dagger\|_{H^s}\le\mathsf R$, and (b) the link condition $\|\hat{\mathcal A}\zeta\|\asymp\|\zeta\|_{H^{-a}}$ on $\mathcal S_n$. **(F-f) needs the spectral condition** $\bar v_\dagger=(\mathcal L^\ast\mathcal L)^\mu\varsigma$, $\|\varsigma\|\le\mathsf R$.

**The step from (a)+(b) to that is Hilbert-scale operator interpolation, it is unstated, and it is available only at $2\mu\le1$.** With $A:=\mathcal L^\ast\mathcal L$ and $B$ the compressed scale operator, (b) is $m^2B\preceq A\preceq M^2B$. The needed claim is $A^{-2\mu}\preceq c^2B^{-2\mu}$; from $A^{-1}\preceq m^{-2}B^{-1}$ one gets $A^{-p}\preceq m^{-2p}B^{-p}$ **only for $0\le p\le1$**, by operator monotonicity of $z\mapsto z^p$ (Löwner/Heinz). Here $p=2\mu$. **This is Engl–Hanke–Neubauer Prop. 8.21 / Cor. 8.22, interpolation range $|\nu|\le1$ — and §8.5 of `engl2000` is the pin H1 itself names.**

**Part H lives at $2\mu>1$:** `eq:muhalf` requires $\mu>\tfrac56$, and `rem:h6bandwidth`(5)'s frontier-clearing configuration has $(\mu,s,a)=(2,3,\tfrac34)$, so **$2\mu=4$ — three units above Heinz's range.**

**And the file contains the proof that the device is unavailable there.** `lem:srctransfer`(ii.a) constructs, by Löwner's divided-difference criterion, a $2\times2$ witness at **every** $p>1$ showing $z\mapsto z^p$ is not operator monotone. **That witness applies verbatim to the step (L5) performs silently.** `lem:srctransfer`(i) — the only place the file states the device — is at $2\mu\le1$ and justifies it with a phrase that names no theorem.

**Two reasons this is substantive, not cosmetic.** **(i)** On $\mathcal S_n$, finite-dimensional, **range membership is vacuous** — every positive-definite operator there is surjective. So **all of (L5)'s content is the uniform-in-$n$ pre-image norm bound, and that bound is precisely the interpolation inequality.** Finite-dimensionality converts the failure from qualitative to quantitative, it does not rescue it. **(ii)** The estimator runs **plain** Landweber and cites the **plain** theorem; the canonical route to source indices above $\tfrac12$ in a Hilbert scale is a **preconditioned** iteration — a different estimator.

**Honest scope, in the block's own `lem:srctransfer`(ii.b) discipline:** *"I am not claiming the conclusion is false. I am flagging that the bridge is unstated, that the framework H1 pins does not supply it at $2\mu>1$, and that the only general device is the one `lem:srctransfer`(ii.a) refutes."*
**Three remedies, in the block's vocabulary:** restrict to $2\mu\le1$ — **excluded by `eq:muhalf`**; add a commutativity/spectral-alignment proviso between $A$ and the compressed scale operator (the analogue of (ii.b)'s diagonal case); or **state (P-a) directly as the spectral source condition** — which also closes A3, **but costs H1's collapse of four smoothness parameters to three, since $\mu$ would no longer be pinned at $s/(2a)$.**
