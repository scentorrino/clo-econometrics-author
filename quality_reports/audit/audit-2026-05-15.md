## Critical findings (escalations)

**1. Commutativity of $\mathsf L_{b_\dagger}$ and $\hat{\mathsf T}'^*_{b_\dagger}\hat{\mathsf T}'_{b_\dagger}$** (regularity R3 / assumption-auditor UNDER-STATED #2).

- **Site:** `results.tex:L173-178`, inside the proof of `lem:propagator` (Round-3 quadratic-residual step).
- **What is asserted:** "$\mathsf L_{b_\dagger}$ commutes with $\hat{\mathsf T}'^*_{b_\dagger}\hat{\mathsf T}'_{b_\dagger}$ to leading order on $\mathcal B_{b_\dagger}$" — used to make the linear-in-$(\hat b_k - b_\dagger)$ piece collapse into a "modified propagator" $\tilde P^\dagger_{m,j}$.
- **What is missing:** Commutativity of two bounded self-adjoint operators on a Hilbert space is a non-trivial structural property. None of `ass:frediff`(ii), `ass:linkcond`, or `ass:rateconv` supplies a spectral-algebra condition forcing $[\mathsf L_{b_\dagger}, \hat{\mathsf T}'^*_{b_\dagger}\hat{\mathsf T}'_{b_\dagger}] \approx 0$.
- **This is a NEW gap introduced by the Round-3 patch (PATCH-R3-G2-b).** Both narrow critics flagged it independently.
- **Two remedies:** (a) add an assumption that $\mathsf L_{b_\dagger}$ lies in the von Neumann algebra generated by $\hat{\mathsf T}'^*_{b_\dagger}\hat{\mathsf T}'_{b_\dagger}$; or (b) drop the commutation step and bound the linear residual directly. Path (b) is safer — Round-3's tightened horizon $M_n = O(\delta_n^{-1})$ likely lets the linear residual absorb without the commutation trick.
